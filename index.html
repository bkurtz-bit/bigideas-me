A private holding company in financial technology.

01 — Position

We build, own and back companies. We hold them for as long as they are worth holding, and we do not discuss them publicly.

02 — How the group works

  • We build from inside

    New companies are started with the group’s own capital and the group’s own operators. Nothing is spun up to be sold.

  • We hold without a clock

    There is no fund, no vintage year and no outside timetable. That changes what a business is allowed to optimise for.

  • We back people we know

    Occasionally we take a position in something run by someone we have already worked with. Rarely otherwise.
